# Layout

All layout is generated by scripts; `.mag` files in `mag/neuron/` are outputs. Rebuild:

```
python3 scripts/layout/ota5p.py
python3 scripts/layout/bias_ref.py
python3 scripts/layout/neuron_core.py
python3 scripts/layout/top.py
CELL=analog_neuron OUT=$PWD/gds/analog_neuron.gds scripts/magic_batch.sh scripts/gds.tcl mag/neuron
```

Verify (per cell): `CELL=<cell> scripts/magic_batch.sh scripts/drc.tcl mag/neuron` and
`scripts/lvs.sh <cell> mag/neuron spice/lvs/<cell>_ref.spice <cell>`.

## Method

- **Devices** come from Magic's SKY130 generators (`magic::gencell`), cached in `mag/neuron/` under
  deterministic names (e.g. `nfet_..._l4_..._w16`). Guard ring per device; body contact on the
  left ring side (`viagl 100`); gate contact on top only (`botc 0`, avoids an unused pad that
  fails metal1 minimum area).
- **Placement and routing** are computed in Python (`scripts/layout/maglib.py`) and written as
  `.mag` text on the 5 nm grid.
- **Routing** (`scripts/layout/router.py`): each terminal rises on metal2 into a channel between
  device rows; each net gets a metal3 track per channel. Track assignment is left-edge with
  vertical constraints (a riser from above and one from below within 0.6 µm force the upper net
  onto the higher track). The router refuses same-side risers of different nets closer than
  0.465 µm. Nets in several channels are joined by metal2 spines.
- **Macros** (`ota5p`, `bias_ref`) are LVS-verified on their own and connected through metal3 port
  extensions to private riser columns.
- **Power grid** (`scripts/layout/top.py`): metal5 rails (VSS bottom, VDD top), metal4 straps every
  10 µm alternating nets, split around the MIM capacitors.

## Floorplan (`neuron_core`, about 162 × 105 µm inside the 180 × 115 µm top cell)

```
  C3   VDD
  row3 PMOS, flipped (gates down), ABBA: PR1 PR2 PK1 PK2 PK2 PK1 PR2 PR1   (each W=32 as 2 x W=16)
  C2
  row2 BIAS | 37-unit xhigh_po array | spines | Cc_S Cc_K (MIM 20 x 20)
  C1
  row1 OTA_S OTA_K | I0_S  WK WS MS MK WS WK  I0_K | Rz (2 + 2 units)
  C0   VSS
```

- **Resistor array:** R_in, R_k, R_out as mirrored S-K-O / O-K-S triplets (centroids within
  0.15 pitch), divider units (9 : 4 : 3) at both ends, dummy ends.
- **Weight mirror:** WK WS MS MK WS WK — each ×2 copy straddles its reference device.
- **MIM capacitors:** bottom plate (metal3) reached through a metal3 tab; top plate (metal4)
  through a metal4 tab and a via3 placed more than 1.34 µm from the capacitor.

## Pins (top cell `analog_neuron`)

| Pin | Layer | Location |
|---|---|---|
| X (input) | metal3 | right edge, x = 179.4, y = 81.27 µm |
| Y (output) | metal3 | right edge, x = 179.4, y = 82.07 µm |
| VDD | metal5 | top rail, y = 111.5 – 114.5 µm |
| VSS | metal5 | bottom rail, y = 0.5 – 3.5 µm |

## Lessons (what LVS caught that DRC could not)

DRC sees no error when metal of two nets touches — it merges. Every short in this layout was found
by LVS: a riser pair crossing tracks from opposite sides (VN/VP), and a via jog landing on a guard
ring (ST/VSS). Both led to permanent router rules rather than one-off fixes.
